Ayers Aquariums – Privacy

Ayers Aquariums is a private app used by Chris Ayers and a small number of invited people to keep records about aquariums.

What we use: when you sign in with Google, the app receives your name and email address. They are used only to check that you are on the approved user list and to record who made each change.

Where it is stored: aquarium records, the user list and a change log are kept in a private Google Sheet owned by Chris Ayers. Your sign-in token is kept in your own browser so you stay signed in, and you can remove it by signing out.

Sharing: your information is not sold, shared with anyone else, or used for advertising.

Questions or removal: ask Chris Ayers to remove you from the user list at any time.
